Why EcoGlow?

Beauty that fits your values and your budget.

Young consumers shouldn’t have to choose between everyday self-care and making more mindful choices. EcoGlow’s concept brings affordability, skin-conscious product design, and packaging innovation together.

Packaging with purpose

Rethinking the package.

Our concept explores packaging made from agricultural starch that is designed to break down under suitable composting conditions, rather than leaving conventional plastic waste behind.

Any “breaks down within 90 days,” hypoallergenic, or “free from harmful chemicals” claim should only be used commercially after appropriate testing and supplier verification.
